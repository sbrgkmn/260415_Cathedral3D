03 Pinnacles & buttresses

E raises each crown. Successive C steps pinch its shoulders into a slender stem. Every peak becomes a single pointed pinnacle in 3D.

C progressively lifts the spans between support planes. Use Side to inspect the pointed openings along the nave, or Plan to inspect the buttress folds.

Buttress shoulders flare at two depth planes, with a recessed waist between them. Pinnacle collars taper in both directions. Connected surfaces close the material around the nave.
